01

Jalour Developments is the development arm of Al Jalal Holding, an Egyptian real estate group specialising in commercial and administrative property.

Since its establishment in 1985, the Group has delivered residential, administrative, commercial and industrial projects in Egypt and beyond.

Under the Jalour platform, the Group brings development, project delivery and property management together in one corporate structure, with a focus on quality of delivery and long-term stewardship of the assets it creates.

02

A new commercial address for El Sherouk.

nurv is conceived as a curated destination for dining, lifestyle, wellness and everyday retail: a place people choose to visit, not simply pass through. The building is planned floor by floor, so that each level has a clear purpose and the whole reads as one unified offer.

The positioning is upscale and calm: clean architecture, generous glazing, an open public plaza and a rooftop running track that gives the building an identity of its own.

Architecture

08
{{MEADIS_LOGO}}

nurv is managed by Meadis Group, Jalour's retail and commercial expertise partner.

Management is structured as a permanent operating function from opening, covering day-to-day building operations, security, waste management and the tenant experience.

Tenant selection follows a planned curation process led by a dedicated tenant mix consultant, so that each floor holds to its intended concept.

  • Professional management by Meadis Group from opening
  • Planned, floor-by-floor tenant curation
  • Building-wide CCTV and 24/7 staffed security
  • Centralised waste management
  • Regular reporting
