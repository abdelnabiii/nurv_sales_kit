### 1.2 Contradictions and risks (decisions needed are marked ►)

1. ► **Two different building designs exist among the "official" visuals.**
   - Design A appears in the sales deck (pp. 1, 2, 10, 11, 18, 19) and Company Profile p. 23. It has a wave roofline, "RESTAURANT" signage and a freestanding nurv. monument sign.
   - Design B is the official render set NURV. - 20260311.pdf (and deck p. 5, right). It has a curved roof canopy and "SHOP" signage.
   - **The three REGALIA .webp images show Design B: the same building, the same camera angles.** So the official render set appears to be shared with, or derived from, a project marketed as REGALIA. The renders PDF itself shows no REGALIA name.
   - Showing two different buildings in one investor document would be misleading.
   - Recommendation: confirm which design is current and use only that one. If Design B is current, confirm it may be shown given the REGALIA association.
2. ► **Units per floor.** The specification says "approximately 11 units per floor". The CAD plans show 11 units on the ground floor, 7 on the 1st and 9 on the 2nd (plus the basement storage rooms). Per the brief, I will state "approximately 11 units per floor" for ground, 1st and 2nd, alongside the flexible structural grid. Please confirm this is acceptable.
3. ► **Floor heights.** The specification gives 5 m for the ground floor and 4 m for the "upper floors", but nothing for the basement. The brief says "all other floors 4 m", so I will show the basement at 4 m. Please confirm.
4. **Tenant labels on the CAD plans conflict with the USPs.** The plans label the 1st floor as offices and co-working and the 2nd as clinics and a lab. The USPs file wins, and the labels are removed in the redraw.
5. **The sales deck's 1st floor** is "The Shopping Experience" (fashion, jewellery, watches, eyewear). The USPs say home comfort retail. The USPs win: the 1st floor is presented as home comfort and lifestyle retail.
6. **The deck's ground floor is "The Elite Dining Experience".** The brief's heading, "The Dining Experience", is used.
7. ► **Meadis Group description.** The specification calls Meadis Group a third-party facilities management company. Company Profile p. 20 presents MEADIS as a commercial and retail expertise partner (retail concepts, brand management). The draft below uses the specification wording plus a generic reference to retail and commercial expertise. Please confirm the wording.
8. ► **Numbers in developer credentials.** The Company Profile relies on dates and figures: 1985, 40 years, milestone years, 135,000 feddans, 22+ brands, 360+ openings. The brief allows "no other numbers". The draft names milestone projects without dates or figures. Please say if the founding year "1985" may be used.
9. **Spelling.** The deck uses "El Shorouk"; the specification and brief use "El Sherouk". The brochure uses **El Sherouk** throughout.
10. **Pending items** in the specification will not be stated: kitchen extraction, grease trap and gas, generator capacity, per-unit power, water and fire reserve, data cabling, recycling, maintenance fee and lease terms. The specification also gives a 3–6 month fit-out window; it is not shown, because the brief allows no other numbers.
11. **"Underground kitchen infrastructure" is Pending** in the specification but asserted as fact in the USPs. Per the brief, it is described only as "purpose-built F&B infrastructure", with no technical specifics.
12. **The CAD plans are drawn at different sheet scales** (Letter vs A3). The redraw normalises all four to one scale using the common footprint. The architect's phone number and e-mail appear on plans 3 and 4 (01060558379 / @yahoo); they and the title blocks will be removed.

### p.14 — 07 Why nurv (asset points only)
1. **A clear destination identity.** Floor-by-floor curation (dining, lifestyle retail, wellness) rather than unrelated tenancies.
2. **Built for F&B.** Purpose-built food-and-beverage infrastructure designed into the building, not retrofitted.
3. **A landmark rooftop.** The On-Air running track is a physical point of distinction.
4. **Operational discipline.** Centralised waste management, separate basement loading and building-wide security.
5. **Adaptable space.** A flexible structural grid allows units to be combined or subdivided.
6. **A visible location.** Main-road frontage in an established El Sherouk residential catchment.
7. **Professional management.** Day-to-day operations by Meadis Group from opening, with regular reporting.
